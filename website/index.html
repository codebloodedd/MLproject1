Modules 1-3 TMDB Movies Movie Success Theme

What makes a movie successful?

This website follows the same movie-success topic across each module, moving from data collection and exploration into deeper pattern finding and supervised prediction.

Topic Background

Movies are more than entertainment. They are cultural events shaped by storytelling, star power, marketing, release timing, and audience attention. A movie can feel successful because people talk about it everywhere, because viewers rate it highly, or because it remains visible long after its release.

This project focuses on how movie characteristics connect to different kinds of success. Genre, release season, runtime, and financial scale can all affect the way a movie reaches audiences and how strongly those audiences respond. Looking at these patterns helps explain why some titles become highly visible while others earn quieter but stronger approval.

The topic also matters because movie success is not one simple idea. A film can be widely seen but only moderately liked, or it can be deeply appreciated without becoming a major mainstream hit. Exploring both attention and reception gives a fuller picture of what success looks like in the movie industry.

By studying these trends, this project aims to connect everyday movie questions to real data. The goal is to better understand what kinds of movies attract attention, what kinds tend to earn strong audience reactions, and how those patterns can support smarter decisions about content, release strategy, and audience expectations.

Questions to Answer

  1. How are popularity and ratings distributed across the sampled movies?
  2. Do higher-rated movies tend to be more popular?
  3. Which genres appear most often in the dataset?
  4. Does runtime relate to ratings or popularity?
  5. Are there differences in success metrics by release timing?
  6. How do vote counts relate to rating stability?
  7. Do higher-budget films show stronger popularity or revenue signals?
  8. Are revenue values highly skewed?
  9. Can the top-25% popularity label be predicted later using supervised models?
  10. Do certain genre combinations appear consistently with higher success?
